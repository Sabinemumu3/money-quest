这个游戏在教什么？What does this game teach?

01

从孩子的问题出发Start with a child's question

六课依次学习钱的限额、需要与想要、钱的进出、按需采购、储蓄和应对变化。每课先看故事和逐步示范，再陪练，最后换情境独立尝试。股票仅作课后拓展。Six lessons cover limited money, needs and wants, money in and out, stock planning, saving and changes. Each has a story, step-by-step demonstration, supported practice and a new challenge. Shares are an optional extra.

02

安全边界Safety boundaries

不使用真实股票、实时行情或真实资金；不收集学校、地址、出生日期或家庭收入。No real stocks, live prices or real money. No collection of school, address, birth date or family income.

03

看看是否真的理解Check understanding together

可以问：“你为什么先买这个？”“换个数字还会吗？”做过练习不等于完全掌握。每次12–15分钟是试测安排，不是统一的注意力上限；允许孩子更慢、暂停和复习。Ask 'Why did you choose this first?' and 'What if the numbers changed?' Completing practice does not prove mastery. 12–15 minutes is a trial session plan, not a universal attention limit. Allow extra time, breaks and review.
