家长中心PARENT CENTRE

这个游戏在教什么？What does this game teach?

01

可检查的学习结果Checkable outcomes

孩子能说出成本、收入和利润的关系；能在预算内采购；知道股价可能上涨或下跌。Children can explain cost, revenue and profit, purchase within a budget, and understand that share prices can rise or fall.

02

安全边界Safety boundaries

不使用真实股票、实时行情或真实资金；不收集学校、地址、出生日期或家庭收入。No real stocks, live prices or real money. No collection of school, address, birth date or family income.

03

一起讨论Talk together

完成后可以问孩子：“利润高就是最好的生意吗？”“分散投资为什么仍可能亏损？”Ask: “Is the highest profit always the best business?” and “Why can a diversified portfolio still lose value?”
